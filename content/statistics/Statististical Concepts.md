At its core, **statistics** is the science of learning from data through mathematical models, probability, and hypothesis testing. Its primary focus is **inference**: understanding relationships between variables, quantifying uncertainty, and explaining _why_ data behaves the way it does.

# Statistics vs Probability

* Statistics is an observed quantity (empirical) on a sample of data
* Probability is a theoretical quantity from an underlying process

# Estimator vs Estimate

**Estimator** is a special statistical model which after receiving data can output an **estimate** - a most likely reflection of model parameters, such as **probability**.
