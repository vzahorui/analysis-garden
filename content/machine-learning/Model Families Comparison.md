## Comparison Matrix

| **Model Category**                                                               | **Primary Strengths**                                                                    | **Data Type Best Suited For**                      | **Automatic Feature Learning?**                                              | **Data Required** | **Interpretability**                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| [[Neural Network\|Neural Networks]]                                              | High capacity, handles complex unstructured data, scalable gradient optimization         | Unstructured (Images, Text, Audio, Video)          | **Yes** (Hierarchical representation)                                        | Large to Massive  | Low (Black-box)                       |
| [[Tree-Based Algorithms\|Tree Ensembles]] (XGBoost, [[Random Forest]])           | Fast training, excellent out-of-the-box accuracy on tabular data, handles missing values | Structured / Tabular (Spreadsheets, SQL databases) | **Partial** (Feature interactions, but no raw spatial/sequential extraction) | Small to Large    | Moderate (Feature Importance, SHAP)   |
| **Kernel Models** _(Kernel [[Support Vector Machine\|SVM]], Gaussian Processes)_ | Strong mathematical guarantees for convex optimization, effective in high dimensions     | Medium-sized structured datasets                   | **No** (Relies on predefined kernel functions)                               | Small to Medium   | Low to Moderate                       |
| [[Linear Models]] ([[Linear Regression]], [[Logistic Regression]])               | Highly interpretable, lightweight, fast training, no hyperparameter tuning needed        | Simple structured data, baseline metrics           | **No** (Requires manual feature engineering)                                 | Small to Large    | **High** (Direct coefficient weights) |
