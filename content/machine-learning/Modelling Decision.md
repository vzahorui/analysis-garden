Early gate on the [[Machine Learning Pipeline]]: given the binding constraint (interpretability, accuracy, or speed), choose *which family* fits before you invest in training.

![[Modelling Decision.excalidraw.svg]]

Then continue the pipeline (split, EDA, preprocess, build) and use [[Model Selection]] for baselines, screening, and tuning against the plan from [[Validation Strategy]] and [[Choice of a Scoring Metric]].
