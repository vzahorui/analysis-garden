This is a default first look at any two numeric variables when studying their relationship (in scope of [[Exploratory Data Analysis Phases#Phase 2 Bivariate analysis]]).

It reveals linearity, heteroscedasticity, clusters, leverage points, non-linear patterns.

## Watch for

Overplotting kills signal above ~10k points. Switch to hexbin or 2D KDE.

Always jitter discrete-ish variables.
