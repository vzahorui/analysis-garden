Use it when you need quartiles and outlier flags quickly.

![[Box Plot-1789920404103.webp]]

It reveals spread, median, skewness (via whisker asymmetry), extreme values. It is less informative than [[Histogram]] but, unlike [[Histogram]], box plot provides summaries of the distribution which makes it good for comparing multiple groups side-by-side.

![[Box Plot-1789920444419.webp]]
## Watch for

Hides bimodality completely. A bimodal distribution and a uniform one can produce identical box plots.
