# Who and What pictures
Typically Who and What are centered around the current or desired stance on a certain matter. 

For example we might want to draw a typical portrait of the consumer of our product and describe the particular product qualities which he values.

![[Customer portrait example.excalidraw.svg]]

We might also draw a list-like portrait of particular elements which constitute us as a vendor.

# How much and how many pictures

* Insightful data is never boring.
* We should always show the fewest possible pictures to make our point.
    * Pick the simplest model to show your point. 
* The addition of low-key anthropomorphic elements where appropriate does add cognitive
engagement (if you’re counting people, go ahead and draw the people.)
