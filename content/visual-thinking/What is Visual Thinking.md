Visual thinking means taking advantage of our innate ability to see — both with our eyes and with our mind’s eye—in order to discover ideas that are otherwise invisible, develop those ideas quickly and intuitively, and then share those ideas with other people in a way that they simply “get.”

Based on the [[6 Types of Problems|type of problem]] you go through a [[4-step Process]] of looking, seeing, imagining and showing. 

![[4-step Process.excalidraw.svg]]

# Frameworks for different phases

During active seeing you employ [[6 Ways of Seeing and Showing]] in order to neatly place it inside your head, and then again when transferring this to other people during the final step of showing.

During imagining it is helpful to apply [[SQVID]] in order to deconstruct the problem and come up with a proper representation to the outer audience.
