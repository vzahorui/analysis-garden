Methodology for imagining in scope of [[4-step Process]] of [[What is Visual Thinking|Visual Thinking]] for future communicating to the others.

Forces our brain into visual action whether we want it to or not.

1. Simple vs Elaborate
2. Quality vs Quantity
3. Vision vs Execution
4. Individual Attributes vs Comparison
5. Delta (change into the future) vs Status Quo (As Is)

For each category you should conceptually be able to draw a picture in both variants and then determine which one better suits the audience.
